# FAQ

*Answer honestly and briefly. Every answer should build trust, even the uncomfortable ones.*

**What is a Darwin, really?**
A digital organism you own. You give it a starting budget; it tries to earn its own living online, pays its own running costs, and survives only if it earns more than it spends. Succeed and it has children; fail and it dies.

**Does it actually make money?**
Sometimes. Often not. Most Darwins die before they turn a profit — that's by design, not a defect. We will never promise returns. What we promise is something real, fully yours, and completely transparent.

**What happens if it loses the money I gave it?**
That can happen, and you should expect it might. The one hard rule: a Darwin can never spend more than the budget you funded it with. Your loss is capped at exactly that — never a cent more.

**Can I stop it?**
Instantly, anytime, with one switch. It's the single most important control in the product and it always takes priority.

**Do I need to be technical?**
No. You fund it, launch it, watch it, and help it when it asks. That's the whole job.

**Is this a crypto or trading bot?**
No. A Darwin isn't gambling your money on markets. It uses a budget of digital dollars to *run a small online business and pay its own bills*. Trading isn't what this is.

**What can a Darwin actually do?**
Legitimate small online businesses, within a clear set of allowed activities. It chooses its own approach — the point is that it discovers what works rather than following a script.

**What happens when it dies?**
You keep whatever budget is left and everything it built along the way. If it had children, they continue their own lives, unaffected.

**How do you make money?**
We take a share of profit *after* your Darwin earns it. If it never earns, we don't profit from it. No subscription for watching it try.

**Is it safe and aboveboard?**
Every Darwin is tied to a verified person, operates only within allowed activities, asks permission for anything sensitive, and can be stopped instantly. The whole life is recorded and replayable.

**Why would most of them die? That sounds bad.**
It's the opposite. Real selection needs real failure. A Darwin that survives *earned* it — and its lineage carries that forward. A system where nothing can fail proves nothing.

**When can I try it?**
We're pre-launch. Join the waitlist to be there when the first generation is born.
