# Trust & control

*The reassurance section. A product that handles money and acts on its own has to earn trust on the page. Plain, calm, specific — never defensive.*

## You are always the owner

A Darwin acts on your behalf, never on its own authority. You're the principal; it's the worker. Everything it does, it does within the boundaries you and its fixed DNA set.

## One switch ends it

You can stop your Darwin at any moment, instantly. No negotiation, no delay, no "are you sure it knows best." It's the most important button in the product, and it always works.

## It can never outspend what you gave it

You set the budget. You can tighten the limits whenever you want. A Darwin physically cannot spend beyond them — not by mistake, not by cleverness, not ever. The most you can lose is exactly what you chose to fund it with. Never more.

## It asks before anything sensitive

Big decisions, identity steps, anything above the limits you set — your Darwin pauses and asks you first. Autonomy stops where your explicit permission is required.

## Your money and your work stay yours

Whatever a Darwin earns is yours. You can have profit sent to you automatically or let it reinvest. When a Darwin dies, you keep whatever was left over *and* everything it built along the way — accounts, work, progress. Nothing is held hostage.

## We only do well when you do

No monthly fee for watching it struggle. We earn a share of profit *after* your Darwin earns it — and nothing if it doesn't. Our incentive is your Darwin working, not your subscription renewing.

## Real people only

Every Darwin is tied to a verified human. It's not anonymous, and it never pretends to be a specific real person. That's not a limitation — it's what makes a system this autonomous safe to operate in the real world.

## We tell you the truth

We will never promise profit. We say plainly that most Darwins die. You can replay any moment of your Darwin's entire life and see exactly what it did and why. If something goes wrong, you'll hear it from us straight.

---

### The trust line, condensed

**You set the limits. It can't exceed them. It asks before anything sensitive. You can stop it instantly. You keep what's yours. And we only win when you do.**
