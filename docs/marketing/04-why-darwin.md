# Why Darwin

*The value proposition. Use as a "what makes it different" section — ideally a comparison or a set of punchy claims.*

## You own it. You don't rent it.

Most AI products are tools you pay to borrow. The platform keeps the leverage; you keep the subscription. A Darwin is different: it's *yours*. It works for you, earns for you, and anything it builds belongs to you — even after it's gone.

## It has skin in the game

A demo can't fail in any way that matters. A Darwin can. It lives on a real budget that can really hit zero. That single fact is why a surviving Darwin means something — it didn't pass a test, it stayed alive.

## It gets better than the day you launched it

A Darwin can improve how it works while it's running, and the ones that succeed pass their lessons to the next generation. You're not stuck with version one. You're watching a lineage compound.

## We're honest about the odds

Most Darwins die. We say that out loud, on purpose, everywhere. We will never promise you profit, because anyone who does is selling you something. What we promise is something real, watchable, and entirely yours — and the truth about how it's going, always.

## Our incentives match yours

We don't charge you a monthly fee to watch your Darwin struggle. We do well when your Darwin does well, and not before. If it never earns, we don't profit from it sitting there. That keeps us pointed at the same goal you are: a Darwin that actually works.

## You are always in control

One switch ends it, instantly. You set the spending limits, and nothing can exceed them. It asks before it does anything sensitive. Owning something this autonomous only works if you can always stop it — so you always can.

### The one-line version

**Everyone else sells you an AI tool. We let you raise an AI organism — one you own, with real stakes, that you can stop at any time, and we only win when you do.**
