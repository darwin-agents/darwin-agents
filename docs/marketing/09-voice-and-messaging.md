# Voice & messaging

*The guide that keeps every page sounding like the same company. Hand this to anyone writing copy.*

## How we sound

**Honest.** We lead with the truth, including the uncomfortable part (most Darwins die). Honesty isn't a disclaimer we bury — it's the most persuasive thing we have.

**Vivid.** This is a living organism with a budget, hunger, children, and a death. We use that language plainly because it's accurate, not because it's cute.

**Grounded.** Confident, never breathless. We describe something genuinely new without a single hype word. If a sentence sounds like a 2015 startup tagline, rewrite it.

**Calm about control.** Autonomy is reassuring only if control is obvious. We never sound defensive about safety — we state it plainly and move on.

## Words we use

organism · lives · earns its keep · skin in the game · budget · metabolism · hunger · evolve · reproduce · lineage · generation · selection · you own it · you're the off switch · honest about the odds · real stakes

## Words we never use

revolutionary · game-changing · disrupt · passive income · get rich · guaranteed · effortless · set it and forget it · 10x · unleash · supercharge · to the moon · financial freedom · while you sleep

Also never: the names of any technology, model, framework, chain, or protocol. Marketing pages describe the *idea*. Implementation lives elsewhere.

## The three things every visitor must leave with

1. **What it is:** a digital organism you own that tries to earn its own living, evolving if it thrives and dying if it doesn't.
2. **Why it's different:** real stakes, real ownership, radical honesty, and you're always in control.
3. **What's true:** most die, we never promise profit, and we only make money when you do.

## Positioning, one sentence

**Everyone else sells an AI tool you rent. We let you raise an AI organism you own — with real stakes, full transparency, an off switch you always hold, and a company that only wins when you do.**

## Taglines (shortlist)

- *Raise a digital organism that earns its own living.*
- *It lives or dies by what it earns.*
- *Not a tool you rent. A life you own.*
- *Real stakes. Real selection. No hype.*
- *Be there when the first generation is born.*

## Social blurb (reusable)

> You don't run a script — you raise an organism. Give a Darwin a budget and it tries to earn its own living online. Thrive and it has children. Fail and it dies. You own it, you watch every move, and you can stop it anytime. Most won't make it. That's the point.

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say most Darwins die | Imply profit is likely |
| Show the life — thinking, trying, failing | Show only the wins |
| Make the off switch prominent | Bury control in fine print |
| Use the organism metaphor literally | Explain how anything works under the hood |
| Cap the downside clearly ("never more than you fund") | Be vague about money |
| Sound like a calm, honest builder | Sound like a hype thread |
