# Elevator pitch

The same idea at every length. Pick the one that fits the slot.

## Five words

**Launch a digital living organism.**

## One sentence

Darwin lets you launch a digital organism that lives on its own budget, tries to earn its keep online, and evolves across generations — or dies trying.

## One paragraph

A Darwin is an autonomous digital organism you own. You give it a starting budget; it explores ways to make money online, pays its own running costs out of that budget, and stays alive only as long as it earns more than it spends. When a Darwin succeeds, it reproduces — passing what it learned to a new generation. When it fails, it dies. You watch its entire life unfold on a living dashboard, help it when it gets stuck, and keep what it earns. No guarantees, no hype: real stakes, real selection, real evolution.

## One tweet

You don't run a script. You raise an organism. Give a Darwin a budget, and it tries to earn its own living online — evolving if it thrives, dying if it doesn't. You own it. You watch it live. You can pull the plug anytime.
